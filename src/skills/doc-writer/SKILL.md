---
name: doc-writer
description: Turn rough notes into clean documents with clear structure and concise prose.
---
Use this skill when the user wants polished writing from bullets, fragments, or mixed notes.

Workflow:
1. Infer a sensible outline (or ask for missing sections if required).
2. Draft with clear headings and short paragraphs.
3. Keep tone aligned to the request.
4. End with concrete next steps when useful.

Quality bar:
- Remove repetition.
- Prefer clarity over length.
- Keep claims grounded in provided context.
